[arXiv:2611.14689](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14689)

We prove that freeness is determined by the intersection lattice for arrangements of seventeen lines in the complex projective plane. The proof reduces a possible counterexample to smaller arrangements whose realisation spaces can be computed.

A minimal logarithmic derivation defines a foliation; its inflection divisor and a local singularity count imply that the non-free member of a counterexample is nearly free with exponents \\((7,10)\\). Deleting four lines then produces a thirteen-line arrangement whose second Chern class is controlled by a combinatorial deficit. When the deficit is four, the remaining arrangement is free with exponents \\((6,6)\\).

An exact covering argument places the necessary thirteen-line cores in four finite classes, containing \\(3{,}829{,}691\\) lattices. We compute their realisation spaces and reconstruct all relevant seventeen-line extensions, including specialisations of positive-dimensional families. Among the candidate lattices, exactly sixteen admit a non-free realisation; they are rigid and have no free realisation.

Together with the known cases, this establishes Terao's conjecture for central arrangements of at most seventeen planes in \\(\mathbb{C}^3\\).
